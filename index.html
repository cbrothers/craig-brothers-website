CHAPTER 01 // CRAIG

How did you find me?

Engineer at heart, farm hand by marriage, and relentless tinkerer.

I've been calling Stephentown, NY home since March 2008. Together with my wife, Karry, we run Emerald Glen Equestrian Center while juggling full-time work. It's a busy life, and it has definitely taught me the value of hard work - especially when I'm outside at midnight in -20F wind chill wrestling with frozen round bales!

Truth be told, the horses were Karry's idea. I didn't grow up around them, so I'm still learning the ropes. While Karry is the equestrian expert, I'm an engineer at heart. I love building things, tinkering, and problem-solving. When I have some free time, you'll usually find me designing websites, configuring network equipment, expanding our home automation and farm telemetry systems, or in my office with a laptop, a bass guitar, and headphones working on the next track.

Health & Resilience

On May the 4th, 2011, I underwent Roux-en-Y gastric bypass surgery at 385 lbs. Losing 150 lbs let me ditch the CPAP machine, get on a horse and learn to ride Gus, get back on skis for the first time in 20 years, and maintain comfortably in the mid-200s for well over a decade.

Between 2023 and 2025, a relentless two-year battle with chronic migraines and failing eyesight sent me through a gauntlet of specialists until a neuro-optometrist solved the puzzle in May 2025: a gradual post-bypass copper and zinc deficiency had been starving my optic nerves, triggering blurred vision and a host of other "fun" neurological side effects. With supplementation dialed back in, life returned to normal... ish? :)

tl;dr: Roux-en-Y gastric bypass patients should never stop taking their prescribed supplements!

CHAPTER 03 // SONGWRITING & PRODUCTION

MUSICAL ROOTS // 1990 – 1996 → 2025+

From a Converted Boiler Room to Late-Night Office Tracks

Long before server racks and cloud migrations, music was the original plan. Under my high school music instructor at Nathan Hale-Ray High School in East Haddam, CT, I practically lived in the school's music room: a converted old boiler room with zero windows, tucked far enough from the rest of the classrooms that we could make all the noise we wanted. That led to an academic scholarship for Music Education at the University of Connecticut (1994–1996), though after two years at UConn I realized traditional classrooms weren't how I was wired. A few years later in 1999, I took a single Pascal course at BCC before deciding to learn code on the job instead. Life detoured into systems engineering for the next two and a half decades, but those musical roots never left, and today they fuel late-night songwriting and production in my office with a laptop, a bass guitar, and a pair of headphones.

CHAPTER 05 // FREYA

Freya - Our shooting "Star"

Adopted Summer 2024 from Homeward Bound Dog Rescue.

Our journey with Freya began in the summer of 2024 when Karry spotted her on the Homeward Bound Dog Rescue website, where she was listed as "Star." Something about her sweet face and bright eyes drew us in immediately, and we knew we had to meet her.

When we arrived at the rescue, we were approved and headed home with her within an hour! They were happy to hear we already had a Russell and a Husky at home, knowing she would be in good company. Once she got here, she quickly made the place her own, getting along wonderfully with both Maya and Kora.

Now, she and Kora are practically glued to each other's hips. Whether they're wrestling, chasing after birds, or just causing adorable chaos, Freya has brought a whole new energy to our home, and we couldn't imagine life without her!

CHAPTER 06 // KORA

Kora - Chloe 2.0*

Flown up from Florida in Spring 2024 with a bark ten times her size.

Several months after the passing of Karry's beloved Jack Russell, Chloe, Karry got the itch again for another Jack Russell, and Maya needed some company. So in the spring of 2024, we welcomed Kora into our lives! She was flown from Florida (Butterball Kennel) to Bradley International Airport, ready to start her new adventure with us.

Kora is a playful, tiny whirlwind of energy with a bark that can startle anyone who isn't expecting it! When she's not zooming around the house or playing with Freya, she has two favorite spots: sitting on the back of Karry's recliner while Karry works, or curling up between my knees to soak up the heat from my laptop.

She may be small, but she has a huge personality and has brought so much warmth and fun into our home!

*WHY "CHLOE 2.0" // IN LOVING MEMORY

The asterisk in "Chloe 2.0*" belongs to Karry's beloved first Jack Russell Terrier, Chloe, and our sweet Husky, Maya. For years, the two of them were inseparable fixtures of the house and barn aisles at Emerald Glen - whether Chloe was chasing a ball at Mach 3, sporting her miniature horse-blanket coat trying not to freeze in the snow, or the two of them were camped out together under the kitchen table waiting for a crumb to drop.

AUGUST 2026 UPDATE // PAIN-FREE & THRIVING

Uveitis, Oakencroft & Karry's Post-Op Sense of Humor

Around 2021, we started to notice Gus's right eye beginning to cloud over. After battling recurring uveitis in that right eye, Gus's eye eventually got bad enough that he made the decision for us that it needed to come out. Poor guy had it removed in August 2026 at Oakencroft Equine Hospital, who were absolutely amazing with him from start to finish.

Gus is adjusting amazingly well to life with only his left eye, and best of all, he is completely out of pain. Naturally, Karry couldn't resist bringing her sense of humor to the "aftermath" of his surgery with a giant googly eye while the farrier worked nearby.
